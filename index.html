QUESTIONS

Frequently asked.

Are your fragrances authentic?

Yes. Éclat Atelier is designed around authentic original fragrances. We do not sell inspired or counterfeit perfume.

What comes in each decant?

Each size is supplied in a dedicated atomiser labelled with the fragrance and volume. Packaging may vary as the atelier evolves.

Which size should I choose?

Choose 2ml for a first impression, 5ml for several wears, or 10ml for travel and regular rotation.

Can I change my order?

Contact the store as soon as possible after checkout. Orders are prepared quickly, so changes cannot be guaranteed once fulfilment begins.

Is checkout live?

This starter site includes a fully working front-end cart and checkout flow. Connect Stripe, PayPal or another payment provider to accept real payments.
